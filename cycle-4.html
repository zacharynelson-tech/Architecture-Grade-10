Cycle 4 — Foundation Systems & Materials

Foundation type is locked in — now it's about the materials that make it real, and getting organized with a full drawing set.

10.5.210.3.1

Learning Objectives

Understand

Explain the differences between common foundation materials (poured concrete, ICF, brick shelf, precast) and reinforcement methods (rebar, WWM).

Apply

Apply appropriate material call-outs and reinforcement notation to a foundation plan.

Create

Create a foundation plan with materials and reinforcement clearly specified.

Lesson Outline · 11 Days

  • Spread Footings & Foundation Walls
    How a spread footing distributes load, and how foundation walls are built above it.
  • Practice: Labeling Foundation Materials
    Guided practice adding material call-outs to a provided foundation wall section.
  • Column Footings
    Isolated footings under columns — how they differ from continuous footings.
  • Foundations on Sloping Ground
    Stepped footings and how foundations adapt to a sloped site.
  • Concrete Slabs on Grade
    Slab components — vapor barrier, reinforcement, control joints.
  • Practice: Slab Detail Labeling
    Guided practice identifying slab components on a provided detail drawing.
  • Foundation Materials Compared
    Poured concrete vs. ICF vs. precast vs. brick shelf — cost, durability, sustainability trade-offs.
  • Building a Sheet Index
    What a complete construction set's sheet index looks like and why organization matters.
  • Practice: Organizing a Sheet Index
    Guided practice building a sheet index for a sample residential project.
  • AutoCAD Practice — Layers
    Organize a foundation plan drawing into correctly named, toggleable layers.
  • Workshop / Synthesis Day
    Supervised work day refining the Foundation Plan and Sheet Index. Preview Cycle 5.

This is a rough outline and is subject to change.
