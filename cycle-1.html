Cycle 1 — Site Analysis & Topography

Establishing the lot. Students read the site before touching a design decision — every choice in later cycles traces back to what happens here.

10.4.210.4.310.2.110.2.2

Learning Objectives

Understand

Explain why a site analysis must occur before design begins, and how contour lines represent elevation change.

Apply

Apply the architect's scale and correct line weights to construct a scaled site diagram.

Create

Create a scaled contour model and annotated site diagram representing a real site's topography.

Big Ideas & Essential Questions

10.4 — Site Plans and Plot Plans
Big Idea(s)
  • Site characteristics must be evaluated early in the design process
  • Every parcel of land is described for legal purposes
  • A structure can be built just about anywhere
Essential Question(s)
  • What are some environmental impacts of site selection?
  • How does site design preserve or enhance the environment?
  • How does a site influence a building’s design?
10.2 — Architectural Drawing Standards
Big Idea(s)
  • It is necessary to understand and be able to properly use architectural drawing standards
  • Proper use of architectural drawing standards will reduce communication and construction errors
Essential Question(s)
  • How can incomplete or misuse of drawing standards affect a job?
  • Are drawing standards the same for all types of drawings?

Lesson Outline · 7 Days

  • Day 1Introduction to Site Analysis
    What goes into a site analysis (property lines, features, vegetation, utilities, soil, sun/wind) and why it happens before design begins. Live GIS/Google Earth walkthrough of a real site.
  • Day 2Site Diagramming Techniques
    Graphic techniques for documenting sun path, wind, noise, views, access. Demonstrate diagramming 3–4 factors on a sample plan; guided class practice.
  • Day 3Intro to Topography & Contour Lines
    What a contour line represents, contour intervals, spacing = slope. Read a real topo map together; quick steep-vs-gradual visual check.
  • Day 4From Contours to Physical Form
    How stacked contour layers become a 3D model of the land. Build the first layers of a sample model live; students start cutting their own first layer.
  • Day 5The Alphabet of Lines
    The 7 standard line types and weights — construction, hidden, phantom, center, object, section, border. Draw all 7 on the board; matching practice on a sample drawing.
  • Day 6Common Drawing Scales
    Reading and applying an architect's scale (builds on 9.1.1). Convert one dimension into 3 common scales; guided practice conversions.
  • Day 7Workshop / Synthesis Day
    Supervised work day — check in on contour models, Acrobat markups, vocab. Cold-call recap of key vocab + site plan vs. plot plan. Preview Cycle 2.

This is a rough outline and is subject to change.
