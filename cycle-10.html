Big Idea(s)

Essential Question(s)
  • Why is documenting work experiences important for career readiness?
  • How can reflecting on our work help us improve and prepare for future opportunities?

Lesson Outline · 7 Days

  • Day 1Review — Site Through Foundation
    Cold-call review of Cycles 1–5 vocabulary and concepts.
  • Day 2Review — Framing Through Roof
    Cold-call review of Cycles 6–8 vocabulary and concepts.
  • Day 3Workshop — Portfolio Assembly
    Supervised work day organizing all drawings into a single exported portfolio file.
  • Day 4Workshop — Portfolio Polish
    Supervised work day cleaning up sheet graphics, title blocks, and labeling consistency.
  • Day 5Workshop — Reflection
    Guided written reflection on growth across the year, technical and employability skills.
  • Day 6AutoCAD Practice — Portfolio Export
    Finalize and export your complete AutoCAD drawing set as a polished PDF portfolio.
  • Day 7Final Wrap-Up
    Final submission of the portfolio-ready drawing set.

This is a rough outline and is subject to change.
