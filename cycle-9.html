Cycle 9 — Capstone Build

No new content — this cycle is dedicated time to pull Cycles 1–8 into one coordinated drawing set and go deep on one architectural element of your choice.

Applied

Learning Objectives

Understand

Explain how each drawing in the set relates to and depends on the others.

Apply

Apply previously learned drafting and modeling skills to complete a coordinated drawing set.

Create

Create a detailed, presentation-quality study of a chosen architectural element.

Big Ideas & Essential Questions

10.11 — Professional Practice in Architecture
Big Idea(s)
  • Professional and technical skills are demonstrated through the process and product of hands-on architectural work
  • Architectural projects provide opportunities to apply core competencies in real-world settings
Essential Question(s)
  • How do professional behaviors and employability skills impact success in Architecture?
  • What practices lead to high-quality workmanship?

Lesson Outline · 10 Days

  • Day 1Capstone Kickoff — Choosing Your Element
    Review the 5 element options (stair, deck, doors/windows, millwork, dormer) and choose which to develop in depth.
  • Day 2Workshop — Base Set Coordination
    Supervised work day checking that site, foundation, floor, wall, and roof drawings all agree with each other.
  • Day 3Workshop — Base Set Coordination
    Continued coordination work — fixing mismatches between sheets.
  • Day 4Chosen Element — Research & Planning
    Revisit your chosen element's earlier reading and plan the detail drawing or model you'll produce.
  • Day 5Workshop — Element Detail Drafting
    Supervised work day drafting your chosen element's detail drawing.
  • Day 6Workshop — Element Detail Drafting
    Continued detail drafting and refinement.
  • Day 7Workshop — Element Model or Render
    Supervised work day building a model or 3D render of your chosen element (Advanced tier).
  • Day 8Peer Review
    Structured peer review of drawing sets — give and receive feedback on completeness and clarity.
  • Day 9Workshop — Final Revisions
    Supervised work day incorporating peer feedback.
  • Day 10Workshop / Wrap-Up
    Final workshop day. Preview Cycle 10 (Review & Portfolio).
