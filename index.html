How a Cycle Works

1

Read & Reflect

Guided reading notes + a short reflection check-in ground you in the material.

2

Practice

Vocabulary, modeling, and software exercises — you choose the depth.

3

Build the Project

Each cycle adds one piece to your year-long residential design.

4

Reflect & Submit

Work gets submitted through Google Classroom — linked from each assignment.

Choosing Your Tier

Minimum
Up to 70

Core task, complete and correct.

Proficiency
Up to 85

Core task plus deeper analysis or connection.

Advanced
Up to 100

Full depth — presentation-ready, extra synthesis.

The tier you choose sets your ceiling for that task — not your score. Attempting Advanced work means 100 is possible, not automatic; it's still graded on how well you execute it. Your cycle grade is the average of what you actually earn across each task.

Reflection Check-Ins are graded Complete/Incomplete and don't factor into that average.
