DSA I — Study Guide & Practice Exam

Semester 1 Final Exam covers Cycles 1–5 (Standards 10.1–10.5). 50 questions on the real exam.

Study Guide

Check off each skill as you feel confident with it — pulled directly from the official exam outline.

10.1Architectural Communication Techniques — Software8 questions on exam

Core software tools used all year: AutoCAD (layers, blocks, object snaps, paper/model space), Adobe Acrobat and Photoshop (markups, file conversion, image editing), and SketchUp (3D modeling with Push/Pull). Review what each tool is for and why CAD improves speed and accuracy over hand-drafting.

10.2Architectural Drawing Standards15 questions on exam

The 'rules of the road' for every drawing: the alphabet of lines, correct scale selection, hatch patterns for materials, annotations (dimensions, tags, callouts), and title block/sheet organization. Know the purpose of each line type and how to pick the right scale for a given drawing.

10.3Architectural Documentation Sets & Basic Blueprint Reading6 questions on exam

Knowing which drawings belong in a set and why. Presentation, working, and construction sets serve different audiences and levels of detail. Match a drawing type to its purpose and know where to find legends and schedules.

10.4Site Plans and Plot Plans12 questions on exam

Everything about the land a building sits on: property lines, setbacks, zoning, topography (contour lines and slope), and building orientation (sun path, environmental impact). Review reading a contour map and how zoning/setbacks limit what can be built.

10.5Residential Foundation Systems9 questions on exam

The three main foundation types (slab, crawlspace, full basement), common materials (concrete, ICF, brick shelf, precast), reinforcement (rebar, WWM), and components (footings, stem walls, vapor barriers, drains). Know why each component exists.
