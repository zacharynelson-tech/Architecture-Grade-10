Cycle 7 — Walls, Insulation, Fireplace, Doors/Windows, Millwork

The house gets a face — materials, openings, and how the wall assembly actually goes together, plus style-defining details like fireplaces and millwork.

10.7.310.7.410.7.510.7.610.9.110.9.410.9.3

Learning Objectives

Understand

Explain how insulation type and wall assembly affect energy efficiency.

Apply

Apply correct line weights and layer conventions to draft a wall section detail.

Create

Create a complete wall section drawing integrating structure, insulation, and finish materials.

Lesson Outline · 10 Days

  • Day 1Wall Framing Methods
    Balloon vs. platform vs. wood stud framing — how walls go up over the floor framing from Cycle 6.
  • Day 2Wall Components & Sheathing
    Studs, plates, blocking, sheathing — the anatomy of a wall assembly.
  • Day 3Thermal Insulation & R-Value
    How insulation type and R-value affect energy performance.
  • Day 4Practice: Comparing Insulation Types
    Guided practice comparing insulation options for cost, R-value, and sustainability.
  • Day 5Moisture Control & Ventilation
    Vapor retarders and ventilation — why walls need to breathe correctly.
  • Day 6Fireplaces & Chimneys
    Fireplace requirements and how a masonry chimney is constructed.
  • Day 7Doors, Windows & Openings
    How door and window openings are framed within a wall assembly.
  • Day 8Millwork & Trim
    Molding, casing, and trim — how finish millwork completes a wall.
  • Day 9AutoCAD Practice — Wall Section
    Draft a wall section drawing showing framing, insulation, and finish layers.
  • Day 10Workshop / Synthesis Day
    Supervised work day finalizing the Wall Section drawing and Elevations. Preview Cycle 8.

This is a rough outline and is subject to change.
