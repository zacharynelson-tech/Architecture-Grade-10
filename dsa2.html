DSA II — Study Guide & Practice Exam

Semester 2 Final Exam covers Cycles 6–10 (Standards 10.6–10.10). 50 questions on the real exam.

Study Guide

Check off each skill as you feel confident with it — pulled directly from the official exam outline.

10.6Residential Framing Systems4 questions on exam

Loads a structure must resist (dead, live, wind, flood, seismic) and the major framing methods (platform, balloon, post-and-beam). Be able to classify a load as fixed or moving and identify a framing method from a diagram.

10.7Residential Assemblies — Floor and Wall13 questions on exam

How floors and walls are built: floor components (subfloor, sill plate, rim joist, blocking, trusses), joist spacing/span tables, actual vs. nominal lumber, wall components (plates, studs, headers), sheathing/insulation, building wrap, and wall section drawings.

10.8Residential Roofing Systems11 questions on exam

Roof component vocabulary (rafters, collar ties, fascia, soffits, ridge boards), reading a roof plan (ridges, hips, valleys, slope notation), and comparing roofing materials for cost and environmental impact.

10.9Architectural Elements18 questions on exam

The heaviest-weighted standard on this exam. Covers stairs (types, components, code-compliant rise/run), millwork/trim (crown molding, base, casing, wainscoting), and exterior elements like dormers, porches, decks, and balconies. Spend extra review time here.

10.10Career Readiness & Employability4 questions on exam

Professional and workplace expectations beyond the drafting table: attendance, hygiene, professional communication, and what employers look for in entry-level candidates.
